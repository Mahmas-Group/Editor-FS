Privacy Policy for Editor FS

Application: Editor FS (com.fsgroupz.editorfs)
Last Updated: October 13, 2026 | Effective Date: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Editor FS ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and storage when you use the Editor FS mobile application on Android.

Editor FS is an on-device video, audio, and photo editing application designed with a privacy-by-default architecture.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Personal Identification Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification.
  • No Account or Authentication Data: Editor FS does not feature user accounts, user logins, or cloud profiles. You use the application completely anonymously.
  • No Financial Information: No credit card numbers, billing information, or payment credentials are collected or processed.
  • No Advertising Identifiers or Trackers: Editor FS does not integrate third-party ad networks, advertising IDs (AAID), tracking pixels, or cross-app analytics SDKs.

3. How Your Media and Files Are Handled

On-Device Processing

All video decoding, trimming, slicing, transitions, audio mixing, color grading, and rendering are executed locally on your device utilizing native Android APIs (MediaCodec, MediaMuxer, OpenGL ES).

  • No Cloud Uploads: Your media clips, draft projects, exported videos, and audio tracks never leave your device. We operate no cloud rendering servers, and we do not transmit your creative content to any remote server.
  • App-Private Storage: Imported media selected through the system file picker is temporarily cached in app-private storage strictly to facilitate editing and rendering performance.
  • Exporting to Device Gallery: When you export your final composition, the video is saved directly to your device's shared MediaStore (Movies/EditorFS) using standard Android MediaStore APIs.

4. Permissions Used and Rationale

Editor FS follows the principle of least privilege. We do not request broad device storage or gallery-scanning permissions.

Permission Purpose / Rationale
android.permission.INTERNET Permitted for runtime font delivery (Google Fonts) and standard Android platform component dependencies. No user data, media, or telemetry is transmitted across the internet.
android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (maxSdkVersion="32") Included strictly for legacy backward compatibility on Android 12 and below when accessing user-selected media. On Android 13+ (API 33+), user media is accessed exclusively via the secure Android System File Picker.

Editor FS does not request: READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, CAMERA, or RECORD_AUDIO.

5. Third-Party Services and SDKs

Editor FS incorporates a minimal set of open-source libraries:

  • Flutter Framework & Engine (Google): Cross-platform UI rendering engine.
  • google_fonts: Retrieves typeface definitions, cached locally on device once fetched.
  • audioplayers / video_player: Android native media playback bindings.

No third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer) or crash-reporting services are compiled into the application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all project data, edits, and cached assets reside solely in your device's local application storage:

  • You may delete any project directly within the Editor FS project list.
  • Clearing the application cache or storage in Android System Settings completely erases all cached project data and temporary working files.
  • Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all internal app-private files and caches.

7. Children's Privacy

Editor FS is a general-audience creative utility. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or share personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because our application collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies with COPPA and GDPR-K regulations.

8. Security

Because your media is processed entirely on-device, your content is shielded by the Android Linux sandbox and application isolation model. No unauthenticated network services, external broadcast listeners, or unencrypted data transmissions exist in the production release.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
